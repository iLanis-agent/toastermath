Toaster math

Nine slices for the family, two slots, and the first batch is cold by the last. Toaster math turns the pile into batches and honest minutes at the counter, the wattage into kWh and cost per year, and the mystery dial into labeled seconds. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Queue plan - slices, slots and cycle time become batches and minutes.
  • Energy plan - watts and daily minutes become kWh and cost per year.
  • Shade plan - the dial step becomes labeled seconds, honestly flagged as a reference.

Labeled norms

Labeled cycle: about 150 seconds for a medium toast. Labeled slots: 2. Labeled dial: 6 steps at a labeled 60 seconds plus 30 a step. Labeled tariff: 0.15 per kWh; put in your own. These are reference values, not rules - your toaster's own dial and manual decide the real numbers.
